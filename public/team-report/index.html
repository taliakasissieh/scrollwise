Load your GoatCounter export

  1. Sign in to your GoatCounter dashboard and open Settings → Export.
  2. Start an export and download the file when it’s ready (JSON ZIP, or CSV if “Individual pageviews” is switched on).
  3. Choose the file below, or drop it here.

No sample numbers are ever shown here: until you load a real export, there is nothing to report.

Reporting these numbers honestly

  • Say where the numbers come from and the dates: “GoatCounter, cookieless, …”.
  • “Visits” are GoatCounter’s unique visits: one person on one device counts once in a session of up to 8 hours. They are not the number of different people.
  • Only visits from links with ?utm_campaign=… can be called poster or QR visits. “Direct” visits can’t be split into QR scans and other visits.
  • Team devices should have counting turned off (Settings → About and privacy) so tests don’t count.
  • Visitors who turned counting off, use Do Not Track, or block the counter aren’t included, so real numbers can be a little higher.
  • Check the totals against the GoatCounter dashboard before using them in the video.
