How people use ScrollWise

Real, anonymous statistics from your GoatCounter export. The file is read only on this device: nothing is uploaded, and this page can’t connect to the internet.

Load your GoatCounter export

  1. Sign in at your GoatCounter address (for example scrollwise.goatcounter.com).
  2. Open Settings → Export/Import, then under Export to JSON choose Start export.
  3. When it’s ready (usually within a minute; GoatCounter also emails you), download it from Last 10 exports.
  4. Choose the downloaded file below, or drop it here. CSV exports also work.

About this data

    For the competition video

    Video-ready images (1920 × 1080), sentences you can read out, and the exact meaning of each number. Everything uses the dates chosen above.

    What the numbers mean (and don’t)

    Visits, not people

    Website visits
    One device opening ScrollWise, in a session of up to 8 hours. Reloading the page doesn’t add a visit.
    Unique visitors
    Not measured. Counting different people would need personal identifiers, which ScrollWise deliberately avoids. The same person on two days counts as two visits.
    Page loads
    Every time the page loaded, including reloads. Only CSV exports include this.

    QR visits vs direct visits

    QR code visits
    Visits from campaign links with “qr” in their name, such as ?utm_campaign=poster-qr. Only these can be called QR or poster visits.
    Direct visits
    Visits with no referrer. They include scans of QR codes without a campaign link, but also typed addresses, bookmarks and some apps, so they can’t be called QR scans.

    Engagement vs proven impact

    Engagement
    What people did in ScrollWise: reaching a stopping point, choosing to step away, finishing an activity.
    Proven improvement
    Not shown here. These numbers don’t prove that anyone’s well-being improved. That needs a study, such as your research mode results.

    Using these numbers honestly

    • Name the source and dates: “GoatCounter, cookieless, …”.
    • Say “visits”, not “people” or “users”.
    • Call only tracked campaign-link visits “QR” or “poster” visits.
    • Present engagement as how ScrollWise was used, not as proof that it improves well-being.
    • The numbers are more likely to be a little low than too high: visitors who switched counting off, use Do Not Track, or block the counter aren’t included, and people sharing a network and an identical phone within 8 hours can count as one visit. Team testing is the main thing that could inflate them.
    • Switch counting off on team devices, and check the totals against the GoatCounter dashboard before recording.
