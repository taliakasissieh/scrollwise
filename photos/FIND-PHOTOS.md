# Photos to find

Generated by `npm run build`. 122 posts are waiting for their main photo, plus 26 optional carousel photos. A post appears in the feed as soon as its main photo is installed.

## How to add a licensed photo

1. Open a search link below (Unsplash or Pexels: both are free to use). Pick a sharp, natural-looking photo that matches the description. **No identifiable faces**: outfits neck-down, from behind or with a phone covering the face. No visible logos or brand names. Avoid photos that look AI-generated.
2. Download it (the largest size is fine: the build resizes and compresses it).
3. Name it after the post, e.g. `post-trench-weather.jpg`, and put it in `photos/licensed/`. Carousel photos end in `-2`, `-3`.
4. Add one row to `photos/licensed/credits.csv`: the file name, the photographer’s name, the licence (`Unsplash License` or `Pexels License`) and the link to the photo’s page.
5. Run `npm run build`. Without a complete credits row, a photo is left out.

Photos the team takes itself are welcome too (with the same rules); use “ScrollWise team” as the author, “Own photo” as the licence and any link to the project.

Or send the photos and their page links to whoever maintains the site (or to Claude) and they can be added for you.
