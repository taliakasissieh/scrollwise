Licensed photos go here, named after their post: post-<post-id>.jpg (carousel photos: post-<post-id>-2.jpg ...).

Every photo needs one row in credits.csv:
  file      the file name, e.g. post-trench-weather.jpg
  author    the photographer's name as shown on the photo's page
  licence   Unsplash License, Pexels License, CC0 1.0, or Own photo (for pictures the team took)
  link      the photo's page, e.g. https://unsplash.com/photos/...
  changes   optional, e.g. cropped

A photo without a complete row is left out of the website. See photos/FIND-PHOTOS.md for what to look for.
